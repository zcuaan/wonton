Staff Supervision

Quick Check-in

Monthly · 5–10 minutes

Ask:

  1. How are you getting on?
  2. Is there anything you need help with?
  3. Is there anything you'd like to learn or improve?
Keep a brief dated note in the private logbook.

Example logbook notes:

All well: 05/11/2026 — Staff happy and settled. No concerns raised. Previous training support helpful.

Support needed: 05/11/2026 — Staff tired in afternoons and would like help with children's communication strategies. Manager to review afternoon duties and share resources; follow up next check-in.
